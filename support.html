Cloud Chomp: The Hungry Sky

Support

Cloud Chomp is a game where Chomp the cloud munches matching treats, math answers, and spelling letters, for kindergarten through 8th grade. It has no ads and no tracking, and it works offline. Published by CreatiqueMedia LLC.

Need help?

Email info@creatiquemedia.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • How do I restore my purchase? Open Cloud Chomp, tap “Cloud Chomp Plus” on the home screen, answer the grown-up question, and tap “Restore purchases”.
  • How do I cancel a subscription? Cancel in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • The objects fall too fast. Open “Speed & practice” on the home screen (or the pause screen) to slow them down, or turn on Practice mode.
  • Is my child's data collected? No. See the Privacy Policy.
